🛡️ SkyBlockHaven Unban Appeal

If you have been banned from SkyBlockHaven and believe the punishment was incorrect or you have changed your behavior, you can submit an unban appeal.

Requirements:

  • Be honest and explain what happened
  • Provide your Minecraft username
  • Explain why you should be unbanned
  • Show that you understand the rules
  • Be respectful when submitting your appeal
Submit Unban Appeal

🚨 SkyBlockHaven Player Report

Report players who are breaking SkyBlockHaven rules, cheating, harassing others, or causing problems in the community.

Requirements:

  • Provide the player's Minecraft username
  • Explain what happened clearly
  • Include proof if available (screenshots/videos)
  • Be honest and respectful
  • Do not submit false reports
Submit Player Report
